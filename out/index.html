EXP-0002 round 3 — connector-ink warp contact sheet

W1 (naturalness) is a human verdict and is not graded here. What to look for: does the red line still read as the same hand as the grey rest shape it came from? The thin blue line is the same warp without any end blending, so the difference between blue and red is exactly what the blend costs.

Final model: similarity warp, plus a Hermite blend at each end whose arc grows with the turn that end has to absorb. Blend length 0.15 of arc, departure stub ratio 1.50, turn factor 7.0, blend cap 0.40 per end, minimum ink radius 12.0 world units. Orange ticks mark where each blend region ends; the orange dotted spur at each anchor is the departure stub. Full algorithm in CANONICAL-ALGORITHM.md, all sweeps in report.txt.

Per D26 an edge anchor's facing is fixed and may oppose the chord; those cases are marked REVERSED and their U-turn is expected behaviour, not a defect. The creator's remedy — switching that end to a centre bind — is rendered in the W7 section at the bottom.
