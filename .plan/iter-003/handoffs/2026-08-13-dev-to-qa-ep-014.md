## How ingest works

1. Press/move: Round 19 map → local `QImage` segments (SRS-EP-01). Selection armed → no `beginStroke`.
2. Release: flush remaining dirty pixels, **then** map samples to world and `applyOp({ type: "append_ink", … })`.
3. Failure (`<2` samples, bad parent, duplicate id): tree unchanged; 0 half-inserted nodes.
4. No `doc_change` enqueue. No undo snapshot. No peer round trip inside the gesture (preview `stroke_*` still outbound).
